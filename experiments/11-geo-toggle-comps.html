The bar menu — where the permission already lives

Keep — as the permission home

The lightest touch: the existing Weather → “Use my location” group becomes “Your location”, and a Near me first toggle joins the pill it already has. One grant feeds both the forecast and the distances; the setting is durable, set-and-forget.

The problem is the hamburger. The friend in Noord never opened this panel — that's how we got the text in the first place. A preference buried here is a preference that doesn't exist for a first-time user.

  • Zero new chrome; extends a group that already exists
  • The honest place for the permission and its explanation
  • Invisible until the menu is opened — fails the discovery test

IRL COMPS — Tinder / Hinge: distance lives in preferences, off the deck. Durable setting, weak discovery. Exactly this trade.

B

The third filter chip — Where joins When × What

Recommended — the toggle's home

The app already speaks a two-axis filter grammar: When (◷ Any time) and What (▦ Everything), each a trigger chip opening a sheet of counted options. Location is the third axis of the same sentence — a ◎ Anywhere trigger opening a Where sheet: the Near me first sort on top, district chips below.

Two laws keep it honest with a thin pool: Near me is a sort (re-ranks, never empties), and district chips carry their counts — “Noord · 4” tells the truth about curation depth instead of serving a silent near-empty deck.

  • Completes a grammar users already learned — no new UI concept
  • Counts make pool thinness visible, not embarrassing
  • Multi-select + summary label (“Noord +1”) matches the other two triggers
  • A third axis of filter state to persist and to reset
◷ Any time ▾ ▦ Everything ▾ ◎ Anywhere ▾ the filter row as it would read — one new trigger, same posture

IRL COMPS — Airbnb: location as one chip in a filter row, map optional. Google Maps: chips over results. Too Good To Go: distance inside the filter sheet, never on the face.

C

Face-level pill — always on, under the top bar

Rejected — costs the face

Maximum discoverability: a persistent ◎ Near me chip in the under-header slot, one tap to re-rank, filled accent when on. Nobody would miss it — including everyone who never asked for it.

It fails WKNDR's own laws. The under-header slot is already contended — the ✨ checkpoint bar, the share nudge and the match prompt take turns there by design (App.tsx: “they share the under-header slot”). And the face rule is one pill of chrome, highest-signal fact only. A permanent utility toggle is exactly the ambient clutter the aura field was built to avoid.

  • Un-missable; one gesture from anywhere
  • Permanent chrome on the calmest surface in the app
  • Collides with the rotating under-header nudges
  • Promotes one lens above weather — the actual thesis

IRL COMPS — Uber Eats / Deliveroo: header location pill, correct for delivery where distance IS the product. Fever: “near me” tab, and the feed reads as listings, not editorial. That's the tell.

D

No toggle — distance as an ambient fact

Ships regardless — the honest layer

The Hara answer: no control at all. Once location is granted (it already is, for weather), proximity becomes a permanent quiet term in rankPicks — near things drift up — and the card detail states the fact: “Ferry from CS + 4 min by bike.” For Noord the ferry line does more than any radius slider; it's the psychological barrier, named and priced.

Alone it can't express intent — “we're in West this afternoon” has nowhere to go, and its effect is hard to validate because nothing visibly switches. It's a layer, not an answer.

  • Zero chrome; pure “show the work” — very WKNDR
  • No thin-pool cliff, ever
  • No user intent; no visible moment to react to

IRL COMPS — Citymapper: “18 min” as editorial honesty. TikTok / Spotify locality: no knob, just ranking. The quiet default everywhere personalization actually works.

the call

B is the toggle's home. D ships regardless.

D first — it's not a placement, it's the honest layer: geocode on the cron, a quiet proximity term in the ranking, “min by bike + ferry” on the detail. It needs no decision and no new UI.

B when you want the control — Where as the third trigger chip completes the sentence the filter row already speaks, and its counts turn the curation-thinness problem from a silent failure into visible honesty. A stays exactly what it is: the permission's home and its explanation. C dies so the face stays calm.
