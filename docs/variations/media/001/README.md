# Field is a verb — motion studies

Each MP4 is 20 seconds at 30 fps, 390×844, Motion 1. These are **offscreen exports of the actual canvas renderer**, without app chrome. They isolate the brief’s “cover the header” question. They are not screen recordings or evidence of iPhone frame rate.

- [Rain falls](cold_wet.mp4)
- [Heat shimmers](hot.mp4)
- [Warm air moves](warm.mp4)
- [Cloud drifts](cool.mp4)
- [A changeable day turns](volatile.mp4)

The live preview is the interaction check: `?dev=1` → Field is a verb. Motion defaults to 0. Increase Motion explicitly; Reduce Motion and a hidden tab suppress animation. The live split weekend renders Saturday on the left and Sunday on the right in chronological order. These are weather-mode illustrations, not an hourly nowcast.

Actual iPhone Safari performance and the recognition test remain graduation gates.
