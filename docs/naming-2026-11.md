an attorney's question.

**The reading.** The said-out-loud vein, with UNWEEK and OUTRO beside it. What Ness likes in WKNDR and
WKNDO is plain speech set as a logotype; the legal problem is that the plain word is "weekend" and that
word is owned. Phrases people say when deciding keep the five-to-seven capitals and the instant read, and
their registers are close to empty. Ordinary words are taken many times over; two-syllable coinages are
either taken or, when the .com is free, weak.
