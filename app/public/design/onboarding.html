WKNDR design review · Nov 2026 / 02 Onboarding

A minute to learn
your taste.

At the moment WKNDR learns taste one swipe at a time, and the only calibration is a dev-only deck of eight archetype cards (Tune WKNDR, ?dev=1). This is the flow that would replace it on first open. There are six short steps, each asking for a different kind of signal, and it ends with a taste card you'd want to keep, which is the moment to ask people to sign in.

↘

The flow

v2 · refined 4 Nov
Arrive→Who's coming · 1 tap→This or that · 6 pairs→Swipe this weekend · 12 cards→When it rains · 1 tap→Where & spend · 2 taps→Your taste card→Sign in (optional)

About 60 to 75 seconds and roughly 22 signals. That's fewer than the ~30-card round in discovery-direction.md, but each one says more: a pair is a direct comparison, and the setup questions cover things a swipe can't (who you're with, how you take rain, how far you'll go). It can be skipped at every step. If you skip it, you land on the deck and learning carries on from your swipes, as it does now.

The system: every step shares one top bar (back, five-part progress, skip), one question size, one button and the same 24px margins. Each choice is a large tap target with a clear selected state. The weather sets the colour only where it means something: the arrival, the rain question and the reveal.

What it needs underneath

The taste model sees three things todaytaste.ts tokensFor reads category, outdoor and weather fit, nothing else. That's why Tune WKNDR's archetypes only ask about categories. Steps 2, 3, 5 and 6 each need a new token.
New tokens
  • with: solo / partner / friends / kids
  • energy: calm / loud, inferred once per pick by the judge
  • time: day / night, from the when field
  • price: free / € / €€ / €€€
  • area: one of 7 neighbourhoods
  • rain: a weight on the weather term for each user
Build order
  1. Make Calibrate (steps 1, 4 and 7) the first-run screen, using the tokens it already reads
  2. Add the pairs and the rain question
  3. Have the judge tag each pick with energy and time, then add steps 2 and 6
  4. Profile and "why this card" last
Keeps the lawEverything still writes only to the person's own taste profile, never to the deck (curation-surfaces.md §2). Signed out, it all stays on the device, as now.
