WKNDR design review · Nov 2026 / 03 Sign-in

Make signing in
worth noticing.

The account works (magic link, free, no password), but you can only reach it through ≡, then a "Sign in" pill under the third heading. Nobody looks there, so nobody learns their saves could follow them. The fix isn't a login wall. It's to offer sign-in at the moments it's worth something, and to keep the signed-in state visible in the header.

01

Today vs. proposed header

Signed-out readers get a clear "Save & sync" chip next to the saves counter. Signed-in readers get their initial with a green sync dot. Either way, the account has a place you can see.

03

The sheet, then the mail, then welcome back

The sign-in sheet starts with what you get from signing in, not with a form. The "check your mail" step has buttons that open the mail apps, so nobody has to go looking for the link. Arriving back in the app shows what came with you.
