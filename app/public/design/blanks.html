Is pulling from Instagram easy?

Partly. The board's drop box already does the hard part for a single post: paste a post link and it pulls the full 1080px photo, no login. What it can't do is start from an account. The blank cards link to profiles (instagram.com/jollofclub), and a logged-out profile page contains no posts. That's the complication you remember. The only sanctioned way in is Meta's business_discovery API.

Your side · about 45 minutes, once
  1. A WKNDR Instagram account, switched to Business or Creator
  2. A Facebook Page for WKNDR, linked to that account. This is the annoying step.
  3. A Meta developer app, which can stay in development mode since only our pipeline uses it, so no app review
  4. A token that doesn't expire (a "system user" in Meta Business Suite). Ordinary tokens die after 60 days.
My side · about half a day
  1. Ingest asks Meta for the venue account's latest posts
  2. Vision picks the best photo that shows the place or the food, not a poster or a selfie
  3. Store our own copy at ingest. Instagram's image links expire and refuse hotlinking.
  4. Credit and link the post, like every other source
What still won't work
  • Accounts that are personal, not Business or Creator. Most venues are business accounts, but not all.
  • Meta changes this API every year or two. When it breaks, cards go back to blank. They're never wrong.
  • The photos belong to the venue. Crediting and linking is the same posture we use everywhere, but it's greyer than a site's own share image.
My take: build A and B first. They need nothing from Meta and fix the problem this week. Do Instagram afterwards, as an upgrade that gives the bundle's openings real photos. The Facebook Page is the only tedious bit.
