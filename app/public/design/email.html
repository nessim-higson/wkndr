WKNDR design review · Nov 2026 / 01 Email

The Friday email.
Four to choose from.

We've drawn sixteen layouts since July and never picked one, because each was a new arrangement of the same email. These four each do a different job. Pick the job first and the layout follows. All four use the real lineup and the real forecast for 7–8 November.

00

What a good blast is

The rules all four keep to. Whichever design wins, these go into brief-*.ts as tests.

One jobHelp someone decide their weekend before they get out of bed. The email isn't the catalogue. It gives a decision and a door into the app.
SmallFive to seven picks at most. At least one pick for each day, chosen for that day's weather. Saturday night gets its own slot.
The inbox line does the workWeather goes in the subject. The best pick goes in the preheader. No "Issue #12", no "Your weekly digest".
Friday 07:30Late enough that the forecast is reliable, early enough to book. Saturday's forecast leads.
Images are a bonusIt should still read with images off and in dark mode. Every photo is cut from the original with its focal point. A pick with no photo gets a type tile, never a grey box.
Every email trains tasteOne tap ("Spot on / Not for me", "Went / Didn't", this-or-that) writes to the account. That's the reason to sign in.

A

The Friday Brief

Recommended to send first

Job: the city's weekend in two minutes. It builds on the V.12.3 rows (the sky band, a cover pick, then numbered rows by day), with three additions: a short lede in WKNDR's voice, chips for "opens today" and "last days", and a dark "you saved 3" panel that only signed-in readers get. It works for everyone on the list, so it can go out this Friday.

B

Three Things

Job: a decision in twenty seconds. Three big pictures, one each for Saturday by day, Saturday night and Sunday. Nothing else, and the app has the rest. It's the most opinionated of the four and the easiest to share by forwarding. It only works if the photos are strong, so it depends on fixing the images (board 04).

C

Tuned for you

The account email

Job: show that the app knows you, and get better at it. Built from the account: your taste in chips, a best match with the reason behind it, "because you saved…", a pick from your Later shelf whose date has come up, one this-or-that question and one "did you go?". Every tap writes to your taste profile. This is the email that makes signing in worth it.

D

The Forecast Plan

Job: show the weather edge. No other listings email can do this. Saturday is laid out by the hour against the temperature, with sunset marked. Sunday has a Plan A and a Plan B, plus an offer to send Saturday night's call as a push notification. That's open question 5 in CLAUDE.md, the pivot notification, as a first step.

The recommendation

Send A on Friday 6 Nov to Ness and partner, the first two confirmed subscribers. A is the default for everyone. Build C next as the version for signed-in readers, since it's what turns the email into a taste engine. Use D's Plan A/Plan B block as a section inside A on weekends where the forecast splits, rather than as its own email. Keep B for growing the list: it's the one to share.
