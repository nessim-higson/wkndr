WKNDR design review · Nov 2026 / 04 Thumbnails

Too many cards
with no photo.

Section 00 is the main problem: the cards that have no photo at all. Sections 01–03 cover a second problem with the photos we do have. We checked every image in the live feed (97 pictured cards, 226 source images). Almost none fail to load. The problem is how thumbnails are made: every small square reuses the large 800×1200 portrait card image and crops it to the centre. For a typical landscape photo that leaves 30% of the picture, ignores the focal point we already store, and downloads about 150 KB for a box 76px wide.

00

The real problem: 21 cards with no photo at all

Fix this first

21 of the 118 live cards (18%) have no photo, and 5 of those are in the first 20 cards you swipe. 19 of the 21 come from Little Black Book's weekend tips. The tips page has no photo for any of its 45 tips, so each card depends on the venue's own website, and for small places that have just opened, that's usually nothing usable. The pipeline is right to leave them blank rather than show a logo. It just has nowhere else to look.

8 cardsThe venue's website has no usable photo. Its share image is a logo, a banner or missing: Dinamo Pizza, Rondo, Vleck, Sea Palace Café, ITADAKI, CABINET at Centrale Markthal, De Poldervogel, Ajax–AZ. Fix: venue photos from Google Places (below).
5 cardsThe only link is Instagram. Yusu Bakery, Tjin's Toko, Dime Slice, Bar François, Flour Shop. Instagram can't be read without Meta's business API (already on the list in STATE.md, waiting on the Meta setup). Fix: Google Places now, Instagram once Meta is set up.
4 cardsThe link goes back to Little Black Book itself. Le Petit French Café, Noordermarkt, Vermut, NoordOogst market. Noordermarkt deserves a fixed photo in the canon, and NoordOogst can borrow the photo from its other card (one borrow is allowed). Fix: canon photo or venue borrow, plus Places.
3 cardsDuplicates that should have been merged. "Must see: Kusama" (a Tiqets link) is the same show as the pictured Stedelijk Kusama card. Museumnacht and Museum Market have pictured I amsterdam records. Fix: widen the duplicate check, so the pictured record wins.
1 cardA broken link. The Poor Man's Rembrandt links to a DuckDuckGo search, so there's no page to take a photo from. The rink card has the same problem. Fix: the pipeline should reject search-engine links.

Update: Google Places photos are dropped, since they're mostly customer phone shots. The designed answer is on board 05.

Dropped · Google Places photosNearly every venue here is on Google Maps with photos. You give it the name and address, it returns the place, and the place's photos come with credits. That covers about 15 of the 21. The cost is a few cents a week at our volume, well inside the free monthly credit. Two catches: Google's terms don't allow storing copies of the photos (so they're served through a small worker), and each photo has to show its photographer's credit. That fits the "credit the source" rule we already follow.
Already designed, still off · SERPEROpen-web image search with the vision check on top. It needs a SERPER_API_KEY secret. It's good for events and weaker for a pizza place that opened last week.
Deck rule, todayWhatever source we add, keep photo-less cards out of the first 3 slots and never put two next to each other. That alone would fix most of the first impression.
When there's still nothing · a map plateEvery venue has an address, so a blank card could show a tinted street map of where it is (OpenStreetMap, credited) in place of the type tile. It's honest, it's useful, and it never looks broken. A Pexels stock photo of "pizza" is the wrong answer, because it shows a place that isn't the real one.

02

What's going wrong, by how many thumbs

~84 of 93Over-cropped. 70 of the 97 sources are landscape. wsrv crops them to 2:3 portrait, then CSS crops that to a centred square. The median thumb shows 30% of the photo. imageFocal is ignored, and the list's parallax moves the crop further.
all 97Heavy and slow to appear. About 150 KB each, roughly 14 MB for the list. background-image can't be lazy-loaded and has no srcset, so rows sit as grey boxes. At start-up the app also preloads every 1600px card face (App.tsx:576), which competes with the thumbs.
21 of 118 · 18%No photo at all. These get the category tile. The keyless DuckDuckGo image search is dead and SERPER_API_KEY isn't set (only PEXELS_API_KEY is).
~9Posters with the text cut off. RA and club flyers, Diwali, the Polish Film Festival, Javaplein.
11 cards · 5 groupsThe same photo on several cards. For example, the Amsterdam Museum image is served from three different URLs, so a URL-based duplicate check (photoHolderAfter) can't catch it. Open PR #38 doesn't either.
3, plus 2 waitingHard failures. Wikimedia returns 429 errors to wsrv (Foodhallen ×2, Melkweg), which then falls back to a 1280px original. Two canon images return 404 (Mata Hari, Bar Fisk). Nothing detects a failed load, so they'd show as grey boxes.
03

The fix, in order

Quick wins · about a day
  1. thumbOf(src, focal, px) in lib/image.ts: originalOf, then w=h=px·2, fit=cover, a=focal (or attention when there's no read), output=webp. Use it at all 8 thumbnail sites. The email briefs already do this (brief-shared.ts:26).
  2. Thumbs become <img loading=lazy decoding=async width height>, with an onError that swaps in NoPhotoFace. Do the same for the card face and detail header.
  3. Preload only the next 3 cards, not all of them.
  4. Poster mode for flyers: fit=contain on a blurred copy.
  5. Add a SERPER_API_KEY to the repo secrets (Ness).
Structural · a week
  1. Store our own copies at ingest. Pre-cut thumb, card and header sizes into R2 or Cloudflare Images. That ends the dependence on wsrv, Wikimedia's 429s, and source images that disappear.
  2. Detect duplicate photos by perceptual hash, not URL, and add a feed audit for it. Then land #38.
  3. Re-run focal reads for the 21 cards stuck at exactly [0.5, 0.5]. Store null when a read fails, so it falls back to attention.
Also foundThe 2 Nov content refresh failed on two LBB parser tests, so the app is still serving the 29 Oct feed, Halloween events included. Separate from images, but it needs fixing before Friday's email.
