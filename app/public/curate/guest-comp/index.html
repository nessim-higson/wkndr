Guest curators

The ten-minute week

A guest starts from the ranked deck.

They put it in their order, cut what they would skip, and add up to five of their own.

Each of their own picks carries a line in their words.

The week lives on a page with their name on it.

The tool is comped three ways. The page once.

The tool, three ways

  1. The listTen rows. Move with arrows, cut, add in a sheet, say why when you want to. The quickest to scan.
  2. Swipe first, then orderThe ten dealt as cards, the app's own gesture. Keep or cut, then the list to order and add. Two steps, each simple.
  3. The week as a letterEvery pick is an entry with its line open. Writing is the main act; move and remove are quiet. One screen from start to Send.

The page

  1. Sanne's page, this weekThe public page at /ams/sanne. One line, the week, the picks in order, each with its line.
  2. Sanne's page, after the weekThe same page once Sunday has passed. It stays readable. What is still on says so.
  3. CuratorsThe index at /ams. This week, next, and every past week.

What is real here

The picks are real. This week's ten are the feed built on 5 October, in the order the deck deals them.

The sample additions are events the same run held back.

The two past weeks are the feeds of 3 and 4 October and 26 and 27 September.

Sanne, Joris, Malika and Daan are not real. Every line in their voice is sample copy.

For sitting with a real person

Open the tool on their phone with ?name=Their name to greet them and start their line empty.

Add ?live=1 to load that week's real ten from the production feed.

When they publish, Copy my week as text puts the whole week on the clipboard.

Any link they paste is kept, and the rest is typed. This comp cannot read live pages.

How to judge it

On a phone first. Then on a wide window.

Do a week in each of the three tools and notice which one you would open again.

Count the taps a week takes you.

Then read Sanne's page as one of her friends would.
