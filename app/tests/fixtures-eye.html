Ulrich Seidl – Über das Leben, die Liebe und den Tod

Featuring a wealth of early, little-known and newly commissioned (film) works by the Austrian filmmaker, photographer and theatre-maker, the exhibition confronts visitors with the lives of a number of seemingly ordinary individuals. On show from 19 September 2026 through 31 January 2027.
