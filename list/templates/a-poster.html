De Parade

Martin Luther Kingpark, Zuid  ·  free entry, shows ticketed

A travelling fairground of theatre tents — 95 makers, food stalls, carousel light. It only stops here once a year. Saturday's cloud breaks around six and the evening comes in at 22° with a clear sky, which is the single best window of the weekend. Go at seven, when the tents start glowing.
