Two dry days, and the good part of each is a different time of day. ͏‌ ͏‌ ͏‌ ͏‌ ͏‌ ͏‌ 
WKNDR • 1–2 Aug
Saturday 24°, Sunday 26°, and not a drop of rain in either.
Which sounds like it needs no advice, and that's the trap. Saturday clouds over at lunch and doesn't clear again until six, so the afternoon is the weak hour and the evening is the best one of the whole weekend. Sunday flips it: bright and direct until about one, then flat and still warm. Two good days, two narrow windows.
Five, in the order the sky wants them.
 
01
De Parade
Ninety-five theatre makers in a fairground of tents, once a year, in the Martin Luther Kingpark. Saturday's cloud breaks around six; go at seven, when the tents start glowing and it stops being a festival and starts being a place.
Zuid  ·  Saturday evening  ·  free to walk in
02
Pluk de Nacht
Open-air cinema on the water at the Stenen Hoofd. Twenty degrees and a clear sky at nine o'clock is the best single hour this weekend has. Bring a blanket anyway.
Stenen Hoofd  ·  Saturday, after dark
03
Dekmantel, closing day
It ends Sunday, and 26° under the trees of the Amsterdamse Bos on the last day is the version of it people are still describing in December.
Amsterdamse Bos  ·  Sunday  ·  ticketed
04
Hortus Botanicus
Sunday's bright window shuts at one, so this is a morning job. The greenhouses are fine in any weather; the garden itself only looks like this in direct sun.
Plantage  ·  Sunday, opens 10:00
05
Martin Parr at Foam
For Sunday afternoon, once the sky has gone flat and white. It closes on the 12th, so this is one of the last two weekends.
Keizersgracht  ·  Sunday  ·  closes 12 Aug
