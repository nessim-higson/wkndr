WKNDR • Brief no. 001
Saturday is 24° and cloudless. Sunday it rains from noon and doesn't stop.
Which makes this an easy week to plan and an easy one to waste. Everything outdoors belongs on Saturday — all of it, stacked, even if that feels greedy. Then hand Sunday over to something with a roof and stop pretending you'll cycle to Zandvoort.
Five, in the order I'd do them.
 
01
De Parade
Ninety-five theatre makers in a fairground of tents, once a year, in the Martin Luther Kingpark. Go at seven — the tents start glowing and it stops being a festival and starts being a place.
Zuid  ·  Saturday  ·  free to walk in
02
Kwaku, Zuidoost
The most genuinely mixed crowd in the city and the only festival where the food is the actual reason. Saturday afternoon, before the sun drops behind the flats.
Nelson Mandelapark  ·  Saturday  ·  free
03
World Press Photo
Where Sunday starts. An hour in De Nieuwe Kerk that you'll still be thinking about on Wednesday. It's quiet after four, and quiet is the right way to see it.
Dam  ·  Sunday  ·  ticketed
04
Martine Gutierrez at Huis Marseille
Best-proportioned rooms in Amsterdam, and this work is good enough to deserve them. Fifteen minutes' walk from the Kerk, so it pairs.
Canals  ·  Sunday  ·  ticketed
05
The Little Big Things
If it's really coming down: a matinee at Carré. A wet Sunday is the only defensible reason to be in a theatre at two in the afternoon.
Carré  ·  Sunday  ·  until 16 Aug
